# Status Effects Update - tell us what you think!

Android version **0.59-android.9** makes your current conditions visible on the
main gameplay screen. Thanks to the players who pointed out how easy it was to
miss hunger and carrying too much!

## Your conditions, at a glance

Hunger, burden, exhaustion and other visible effects now appear under your
stats, without needing to open a menu. The labels sit at the **top-left of the
map in portrait** and the **top-right in landscape**.

- **Red:** dangerous conditions, such as severe hunger, poisoning and overload.
- **Amber:** warnings, such as being hungry, burdened, exhausted or slowed.
- **Green:** helpful effects, such as haste or levitation.
- **Gray:** other states and ongoing actions.

The labels keep their names as well as their colors, and urgent conditions are
shown first. If there are more effects than fit, tap **MORE** to cycle through
them. Checking effects does not take a turn.

This area stays reserved even when you have no effects. The map and controls
do not move when an effect appears, disappears or changes page. The effects
stay separate from the map and movement buttons.

## Please try it and leave feedback

We would love to hear how this works on your phone or tablet:

- Does the placement feel right in portrait and landscape?
- Are the labels readable and useful during play, without taking too much map space?
- Do hunger, carrying weight and other effects appear and clear when you expect?
- If you have several effects, does **MORE** let you check all of them easily?
- Do the map and controls stay still as your conditions change?

Please leave a comment with your device, screen orientation and what happened.
A screenshot is especially helpful for layout problems.

## Updating

Download **ivan-android-0.59-android.9.apk** from this page and install it over
your existing signed itch build; there is no need to uninstall first. As
always, back up important saves before updating. This is an Android interface
update, not a change to the game's hunger, burden or combat rules.
