# Readability and Stability Update

This update brings clearer descriptions and safer adventuring to Android and
the enhanced Windows desktop version of IVAN.

## Read the whole description

On Android, long item descriptions now scroll inside their own panel instead
of cutting off. The item title and action buttons stay in place while you read,
including on smaller screens and in landscape.

Long inventory and crafting descriptions also have paragraph breaks, making
them easier to read on both Android and the enhanced desktop interface.

## A clearer view of the gods

The prayer screen now separates each god's alignment and prayer history into
readable sections. With the extra god-information option enabled, remembered
responses appear in their own section. Long information can be scrolled on
Android without accidentally confirming a prayer.

## Safer equipment changes

Quick-equip now checks that the body part and equipment slot are usable before
moving an item. This fixes crashes when equipping from inventory or pickup
with a missing limb, and leaves your items alone when an equipment change
cannot be made.

## Android Back and reopening fixes

Android's Back action now cancels menus and number prompts consistently.
We've also fixed reported startup and reopening crashes, and improved handling
of display changes and Android replacing the game window.

## Windows players get the shared improvements too

The refreshed Windows download includes the description paragraph improvements,
clearer god previews, equipment fixes, and the Origins character starts in the
enhanced interface. The classic interface remains available.

Choose from six balanced Banana Origins, from the strong Plantation Porter
to the quick Canopy Scout or the dog-accompanied Kennel Keeper. For a harder
start, try the Barefoot Courier or Overworked Picker challenges. Prefer to
build your own adventurer? Custom Colonist lets you allocate your attributes
and choose a starting supply loadout. Classic still offers the original
randomized start, and the Hall of Fame can filter scores by character mode.

## Updating

Android: install the new APK over your existing installation to keep your saves.

Windows: extract the new ZIP to a fresh folder. If you want to continue an
existing adventure, copy your old `Save` folder into the new `ivan` folder;
keep the original folder as a backup.

Thanks to everyone reporting issues and helping improve the port!
